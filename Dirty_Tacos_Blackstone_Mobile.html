🌮 Dirty Tacos on the Blackstone

Ribeye · smoked sausage · crispy cheese · jalapeño cilantro crema
Prep: 20 min
Cook: 20 min
Makes: 10–12 tacos
Griddle: 425°F

Cooking steps

Tap each checkbox as you complete a step.
1

Season the steak

Toss cubed ribeye with dark soy sauce, 1 tablespoon lime juice, Heifer Dust, and 1 teaspoon Fiesta Dust. Refrigerate while preparing the other ingredients.

2

Make the crema

Blend Mexican table cream, jalapeño, cilantro, avocado, and 2 tablespoons lime juice until smooth. Season to taste. Add more lime juice for a drizzleable consistency and refrigerate.

3

Preheat the Blackstone

Heat the griddle over medium heat to about 425°F. Leave a cooler area for holding cooked filling.

4

Cook vegetables and sausage

Add a thin layer of oil if needed. Cook onion, bell pepper, and sausage, tossing occasionally, until vegetables soften and sausage browns, about 5–7 minutes. Lightly season vegetables with Heifer Dust and move to the cooler area.

5

Sear the ribeye

Spread steak on the hot griddle in a single layer, leaving excess marinade behind. Sear before turning, then cook until browned and to your preferred doneness.

6

Combine the filling

Toss steak, sausage, onion, and pepper together. Add the remaining 1 teaspoon Fiesta Dust, adjust to taste, and move to the cooler side.

7

Lower the heat

Turn the taco cooking area to low or briefly switch those burners off. Allow the surface to cool so the cheese can crisp without burning.

8

Make cheese skirts

In batches, sprinkle about 1/4 cup shredded cheese directly onto the griddle per taco, forming a circle slightly larger than a tortilla. Place a tortilla on each cheese circle and press gently. Cook until cheese edges are golden and release easily.

9

Fill and fold

Warm a portion of filling beside each tortilla with extra cheese if desired. Lift and flip tortilla with its attached cheese skirt, cheese-side up. Spoon filling onto one half and fold into a taco with crispy cheese inside.

10

Finish and serve

Transfer to a platter. Top with sliced avocado, jalapeño cilantro crema, and fresh cilantro. Serve immediately.

Griddle tips

Let the griddle cool before making the cheese skirts, or the cheese may burn.
For milder crema, remove the jalapeño seeds and inner ribs.
Taste before adding salt: soy sauce, sausage, cheese, and seasoning blends already contribute salt.
