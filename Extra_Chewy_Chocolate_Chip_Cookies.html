Chocolate Chip Cookies — Extra Chewy

🌡️ 375°F❄️ Chill at least 24 hours⏱️ Bake 12–15 minutes🍪 1½-oz portions
Plan ahead: The dough needs at least 24 hours in the refrigerator before baking.

Ingredients

Instructions

Step 1

Melt the butter, add sugars and beat with the paddle attachment on medium speed for a while.

Step 2

Slow the mixer to "stir" and add eggs, milk, and vanilla.

Step 3

Add flour, salt, baking soda, mix for a few minutes, scrape down sides of bowl to make sure everything mixes.

Step 4

Add chocolate, mix in.

Step 5

Put the dough in the fridge for at least 24 hours.

Step 6

Heat the oven to 375 degrees F and place the racks in the top third and bottom third of the oven.

Step 7

Scoop the dough into 1 1/2-ounce portions onto parchment paper-lined half sheet pans.

Step 8

Bake for 12-15 minutes.
