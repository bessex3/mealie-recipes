Old-Fashioned Possum Pie

A shortbread pecan crust layered with sweetened cream cheese, homemade chocolate pudding and whipped cream, finished with pecans and chocolate shavings.

9-inch pie · 350°F · Crust: 10 minutes

Ingredients

Shortbread pecan crust

Homemade chocolate pudding

Cream cheese filling

Whipped cream topping

Instructions

  1. Prepare the chocolate pudding

    Whisk cornstarch, cocoa, salt and espresso powder together. Whisk in half-and-half. In a saucepan combine milk, granulated sugar, chocolate and the cornstarch mixture. Bring to a boil while whisking continuously until thickened. Remove from heat, whisk in butter and vanilla, and set aside to cool.

  2. Prepare the crust

    Preheat oven to 350°F. Combine cookie crumbs, melted butter, brown sugar and some of the chopped pecans, reserving the remainder for topping. Press into the bottom and sides of a 9-inch pie dish. Bake approximately 10 minutes and cool.

  3. Prepare cream cheese filling

    Beat room-temperature cream cheese, powdered sugar and vanilla until smooth.

  4. Assemble the layers

    Spread cream cheese filling over the cooled crust, then spread cooled chocolate pudding over the filling.

  5. Whip the cream

    Beat heavy cream, powdered sugar and vanilla until the cream holds its shape.

  6. Finish and chill

    Spread whipped cream over pudding. Sprinkle with reserved pecans and shave chocolate over the top using a vegetable peeler. Refrigerate before slicing and serving.

Recipe notes

The pecan split, refrigeration duration and serving count were not specified. The crust is baked; the assembled pie is not.
