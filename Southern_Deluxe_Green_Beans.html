Southern Deluxe Green Beans

Sweet and savory Southern-style green beans with smoked bacon, garlic and a brown sugar glaze.

375°F · Bake 40 minutes · 7 × 12 or 9 × 13-inch dish

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven

    Preheat the oven to 375°F. Use a 7 × 12-inch casserole dish or a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.

  2. Prepare the green beans

    Spread the green beans evenly in the casserole dish.

  3. Make the sauce

    Mix the brown sugar, soy sauce, minced garlic, chicken bouillon powder and Worcestershire sauce in a bowl.

  4. Coat the green beans

    Pour the sauce over the green beans and stir until evenly coated.

  5. Cook the bacon

    Fry the smoked bacon in a skillet until crispy and brown. Coarsely chop it.

  6. Add the bacon

    Sprinkle the bacon over the green beans and stir lightly to coat it with sauce.

  7. Bake covered

    Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 20 minutes.

  8. Bake uncovered

    Remove the foil, stir and return to the oven uncovered for another 20 minutes.

  9. Serve

    Remove from the oven and serve warm.

Recipe notes
