Spaghetti with Beef & Italian Sausage Meat Sauce

Spaghetti with a rich tomato meat sauce, ground beef, Italian sausage, vegetables and butter.

Simmer 15 minutes · Pasta 10–11 minutes

Ingredients

Meat sauce

Pasta

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef

    Heat a little olive oil in a large skillet. Add the ground beef, break it apart and cook until browned.

  2. Add the Italian sausage

    Break the sausage apart and continue cooking.

  3. Add the vegetables

    Stir in the onion, bell pepper and garlic. Cook until the vegetables soften and the meat is fully cooked.

  4. Season the mixture

    Add diced tomatoes, salt, pepper and Italian seasoning.

  5. Build the sauce

    Stir in both cans of tomato paste and both packets of spaghetti sauce seasoning.

  6. Add water

    Pour in 3 1/2 cups water and whisk until combined.

  7. Finish the sauce

    Stir in butter and a pinch of sugar.

  8. Simmer

    Cover and simmer on low for approximately 15 minutes.

  9. Cook the spaghetti

    Bring salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti for about 10 minutes for al dente or an extra minute for softer pasta, adjusting for package directions.

  10. Serve

    Drain the spaghetti and serve with the meat sauce.
